# microcopy — Claude.ai prompt template

Paste this into Claude.ai when writing UI text for any section.
Ensures consistent voice across the whole site.

---

```
# Microcopy — quang-studio voice

## My voice
Tone: confident, direct, a little dry — a designer who ships, not one who talks
Register: conversational but not casual — no slang, no filler words
Personality: precise, self-aware, slightly playful in small doses
Avoid: "passionate about", "I love to", "let's connect!", corporate buzzwords,
       exclamation marks in body text, vague words like "innovative" or "creative"

## Context
Section I'm writing copy for: [e.g. Hero / About / Work / Contact / Error page]
Audience: [e.g. product design recruiter / design lead / startup founder]
Goal of the copy: [e.g. get them to look at my work / get them to contact me]

## What I need copy for
(fill in what applies)

### Hero
- Headline (8 words max — what I do + for whom):
- Subheadline (1 sentence — what makes me different):
- CTA button label:

### About
- Opening line (the hook — not "Hi, I'm Quang"):
- What I actually do day to day (2–3 sentences):
- What I care about beyond the job (1 sentence):

### Work section
- Section label/eyebrow:
- Empty state (no projects loaded): 
- "View all work" CTA:

### Contact
- Headline (not "Get in touch"):
- Subheadline (why they should reach out):
- Form submit button label:
- Success message after send:
- Error message if send fails:

### Navigation
- Work label:
- About label:
- Contact label:
- Back to home:

### 404 page
- Headline:
- Body (1 sentence):
- CTA:

## Rules
- Sentence case everywhere — no Title Case
- No full stops on standalone labels or buttons
- Full stops on full sentences in body text
- Vietnamese name: Quang Anh Trần — use this exact form if name appears
- Max 1 exclamation mark across the entire site

## Output format
For each piece: show 2–3 options with a short note on what each one prioritises.
Then recommend one.
```

---

## When to use
- Before finalising any text in Figma
- When copy feels bland or generic
- Before launching — audit all text in one session

## Tip
After getting options, paste the chosen copy directly into your Figma frames
before handing off to code. Copy is part of the design.
